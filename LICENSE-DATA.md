article are governed by the agreement signed with the publisher, not by the licences in this

## A note on the LLM-generated registries

The descriptor text in `descriptors*/` was generated by large language models (Claude Sonnet 5
for the source-grounded registry, Claude Opus 5 for CuPL+ and DCLIP+; see Section 3.4 of the
article). The copyright status of purely machine-generated text is unsettled in several
jurisdictions. The CC BY 4.0 grant above is offered over whatever rights the authors hold in the
registries as compiled, curated and arranged, and is not a claim that every sentence in them is
a copyrightable work.
