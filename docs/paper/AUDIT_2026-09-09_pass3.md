## One new minor, and one residual note

**M1 — "nor can any classifier" is a universal claim the paper itself refutes.**
Abstract, second clause:

> "…bill per query and need connectivity; **nor can any classifier** name a disease on a
> crop absent from its training set."

The paper then presents a descriptor head that does exactly this. The intended reading —
*conventional closed-set classifier* — is clear from context, and the earlier phrasing
("a classifier cannot…") carried the same idea more loosely. But "any classifier" states it
universally, in the sentence that sets up the contribution. A word like "conventional" or
"closed-set" removes the snag at no cost.

**Residual, not a finding.** `lu2025vlmready` (arXiv 2512.15977) now carries an author list

confirm the arXiv ID from this machine. The bib note explains why the other two went but

submission, purely because this manuscript's argument makes citation provenance
load-bearing.
