## New findings

### NEW-1 — Methods still declares the analysis plan the Results now disowns *(major)*
The C1 fix landed in Results but not in Methods. §3.4 (`main.tex:380–382`) still reads:

> "**Encoders serve as the replication unit**: each of the four deployable tiers is **an
> independent test of the same claim**, and we report the per-encoder difference, the mean,
> and a confidence interval over encoders."

§5.3 (`main.tex:490–493`) now says the opposite, in almost the same words:

> "The randomised unit here is the generation seed, not the encoder: the four encoders score
> the same seven descriptor sets on the same images, so they are four readouts of one
> experiment rather than **four independent replications**."

A reviewer reads Methods before Results, so they meet the discarded plan first and the
correction second. Worse, "encoders serve as the replication unit" now reads as the
*pre-specified* analysis, making the seed-level interval look like a post-hoc switch after
the encoder interval failed to survive scrutiny — the opposite of what actually happened.
Rewrite §3.4 to pre-specify the seed as the unit and to state that the per-encoder
agreement is reported as supporting evidence, not as the inferential test.

Also stale: the abstract verification comment at `main.tex:128` still records
`control arm +1.96 pp, 95% CI [+1.70, +2.22]` as the checked value, though the abstract
itself is now correct.

### NEW-2 — two new references have no authors and render as `[1] ,` and `[2] ,` *(major)*
`leafnet2026` and `tinyvlm2026` in `tex/refs.bib` have **no `author` field**. All 23 other
entries do. Because `sort&compress` orders by author, the empty ones sort to the front, so
the compiled bibliography opens with:

```
[1] , 2026. LeafNet and LeafBench: A large-scale dataset and benchmark
    for plant disease vision–language understanding. arXiv preprint arXiv:2602.13662.
[2] , 2026. TinyVLM: Zero-shot vision–language inference on microcontrollers.
    arXiv preprint arXiv:2603.00136.
```

Those are the first two entries an editor sees in the reference list.

Beyond the formatting, I could not verify either record, and a title-plus-arXiv-ID with no
author list is exactly the shape an unverified citation takes. The bib comment asserts
"none is invented", and I am not claiming otherwise — but this manuscript's central
contribution is *source-grounded, auditable* descriptors, with a whole limitations
subsection distinguishing page-verified from model-recalled provenance. An unverifiable
citation in that paper is a disproportionate risk: it hands a reviewer the exact rhetorical
move the paper is built to resist.

One is also load-bearing. `main.tex:230` uses it for a factual claim about a third party's
model:

> "the LeafNet/LeafBench corpus~\citep{leafnet2026} underpins the leaf-disease VLM we
> evaluate in Section~\ref{sec:bakeoff}"

That asserts SCOLD's training provenance. If the attribution is wrong it misdescribes
another group's work, in the same subsection where you already concede your SCOLD wrapper
is a best-effort load. Verify both records against the live arXiv listing, add full author
lists, and drop anything that does not resolve. Apply the same pass to `lu2025vlmready`
(arXiv 2512.15977), which I also could not confirm.

### NEW-3 — the Pareto figure title contradicts its own plotted data *(moderate)*
`make_figures.py:454` hard-codes:

> `"Real-time Pareto: 11M S0 = 15.8 ms/img (~63 img/s) at ~equal accuracy"`

But the same function plots S0 from `EDGE[0][3] = 17.35` (`make_figures.py:127`), Table 5
reports 17.4 ms, and §5.7 and the Conclusion both say 17.4 ms (~58 img/s). The rendered
figure shows its point at ~17.3 on the x-axis under a title claiming 15.8.

This is the B3 bug class again — a number typed into `make_figures.py` rather than read from
a result file — and it survived the B3 fix because that fix only added `_is_reference()` to
the descriptor path. Worth a sweep for other hard-coded strings in figure titles: `EDGE`
(line 126) and `PROBE` (line 28) are both literal tables, so any title text derived from
them by hand can drift the same way.
