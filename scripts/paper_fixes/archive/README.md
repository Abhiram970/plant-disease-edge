# Archived fix rounds

These scripts applied the audit passes of 2026-09-10, 2026-09-11 and 2026-09-12 to the manuscript
as it was laid out then (`docs/paper/tex/`). They are kept as a record of what each round changed
and are **not** runnable against the current layout, where the submitted manuscript lives at
`docs/paper/manuscript/submitted/` and the revision at `docs/paper/manuscript/revision/`.

The live equivalents are `apply_revision_text.py` and `apply_revision_results.py` in the parent
directory. `docs/paper/AUDIT_HISTORY.md` is the ledger that ties each pass to its fix round.
